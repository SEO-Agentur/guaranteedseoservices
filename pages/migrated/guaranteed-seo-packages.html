FAQ

Clear answers before you choose.

What does a “push” include?

Your proposal defines the specific work, priorities and deliverables for each execution cycle.

Can I target a page other than my homepage?

Yes. Every package is priced per URL — you choose the homepage or any other page that matters most to your business.

What is included in the PR campaign?

The monthly packages include PR and authority-building activity for your target URL. The exact format and scope are set out in your proposal.

Does a package guarantee a specific ranking?

No one can promise a specific position on a search engine. What we commit to — scope, cadence, measurement and any remedies — is defined in writing before work begins.

Can I change my package?

Yes. You can start with the Test Push and move to a monthly package, or adjust your cadence later. Changes are confirmed in writing.

What terms apply before work begins?

Before work begins you receive a proposal with the agreed scope, start date, KPIs, client responsibilities and applicable terms.
