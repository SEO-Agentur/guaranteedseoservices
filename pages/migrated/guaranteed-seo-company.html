Evidence Verification

How to validate case studies and client claims.

  • Request specific examples and references
  • Ask for access to relevant data or reports
  • Check if the case is comparable to your business
  • Look for long-term relationships, not one-off wins
  • Verify that they explain methodology, not just results

Warning Signs

Be cautious if an SEO company:

  • Guarantees #1 rankings for specific keywords
  • Promises exact traffic or revenue numbers
  • Uses vague or unverifiable case studies
  • Relies on undisclosed link-building tactics
  • Does not give you access to key accounts
  • Avoids clear contracts or reporting terms

15 Questions to Ask an SEO Company

Use these questions during your evaluation process.

1What is your SEO strategy for our website?
A good answer refers to your site specifically – current visibility, technical issues, competitors and priorities – not a generic package description.
2Which specific deliverables are included?
Expect a list of concrete outputs per month (e.g. audits, fixes, pages, links, reports) rather than “ongoing optimisation”.
3How do you measure progress?
Look for agreed KPIs such as rankings for target URLs, organic traffic, leads and revenue, measured in your own Search Console and GA4.
4What tools and data sources do you use?
Professional providers combine first-party data (GSC, GA4, server logs) with crawlers and rank trackers – and can explain why.
5How do you handle algorithm updates?
They should monitor updates, explain impact honestly and adapt the plan – without panic or risky shortcuts.
View all 15 questions
6What exactly does your guarantee cover?
Ask for the metric, the timeframe and what happens if it is not met – in writing.
7Who will work on our account day to day?
Know whether work is done in-house, by named specialists, or outsourced.
8How do you build links?
They should describe sources and methods openly and avoid paid link networks.
9Can we see comparable case studies?
Ask for examples in a similar industry, market and site size, ideally with references.
10Will we own all accounts, content and data?
The answer should be an unconditional yes.
11How often will you report, and what will reports include?
Monthly reporting with work completed, results and next steps is a sensible minimum.
12What do you need from our team?
Clarify access, approvals and developer time so delays aren’t blamed on you later.
13What is the minimum contract term and notice period?
Flexible terms show confidence in the results they deliver.
14How long until we should expect results?
Honest providers give a realistic range and explain what drives it.
15What happens if we decide to leave?
Expect a clean handover of all work, documentation and access.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if an SEO company is legitimate?

Check for verifiable case studies, client references, transparent methods, clear contracts and that you keep ownership of all accounts. Be wary of guaranteed #1 rankings.

What questions are most important to ask?

Ask about specific deliverables, how progress is measured, link-building methods, account ownership and what the guarantee actually covers.

How do I compare different proposals?

Use a weighted scorecard: rate each provider on experience, deliverables, technical competence, reporting, contract terms and commercial fit, then compare the totals alongside price.

Should I choose a local or international provider?

Choose based on expertise and fit for your market. Local knowledge helps for local SEO; for international or multilingual sites, proven international SEO experience matters more than location.

Can I switch SEO providers later?

Yes – as long as you own your accounts, content and data. Check notice periods and handover terms before you sign.
