Red flags in ranking promises

Be Cautious of These Claims

  • Guaranteed #1 rankings for any keyword
  • Fixed timelines for first-page rankings
  • Guarantees based on exact traffic numbers
  • No explanation of the strategy or deliverables
  • Use of risky link-building or black-hat tactics
Signs of a trustworthy approach
  • Transparent keyword analysis and prioritisation
  • Focus on commercially relevant keywords
  • Clear explanation of deliverables and methods
  • Realistic timelines and dependencies
  • Regular reporting and continuous optimisation

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really guarantee first-page rankings?

No one can honestly guarantee a specific Google position, because rankings depend on Google's algorithms, competitors and searcher behaviour. What can be guaranteed is a transparent process, agreed deliverables and honest reporting. Be cautious of anyone promising a fixed position.

How long does it take to rank on the first page?

For low-competition keywords on an established site, movement can appear within a few weeks to three months. Competitive commercial keywords typically take 6–12 months or longer, depending on domain authority, content quality and how much the competition invests.

What factors influence my rankings the most?

Relevance to search intent, content quality and depth, technical accessibility, internal linking, page experience and the authority of your site (including backlinks) are the core factors. The weight of each varies by keyword and SERP.

Do rankings automatically lead to more sales?

No. A ranking only drives business results if the keyword has commercial intent, the SERP leaves room for organic clicks and the landing page converts. That is why we evaluate keyword value, not just ranking difficulty.
