  • Website migrations or major redesigns
  • Seasonal fluctuations in search demand
  • Algorithm updates or SERP feature changes
  • Increased competition or new market entrants
  • Client-side changes to content or strategy

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about SEO results, measurement and conditions.

How long does it take to see measurable SEO results?

Early signals such as impressions, indexation and ranking movements often appear within 2–3 months. Meaningful changes in non-branded traffic and leads usually take 4–12 months, depending on competition, the starting point and how quickly recommendations are implemented.

What happens if the agreed KPI is not reached?

We review the data together against the agreed baseline and conditions, identify the causes (for example tracking changes, seasonality or delayed implementation) and agree on next steps. Any specific commitments are defined in writing before the project starts.

Which KPIs should I focus on for my business?

It depends on your business model. Lead-generation sites usually focus on non-branded traffic and qualified enquiries, e-commerce sites on transactions and revenue, and new brands on visibility and impressions. We define a primary KPI plus supporting leading indicators.

How do you handle tracking changes or data issues?

We document every tracking, consent or site change with annotations and, where needed, adjust the baseline so that periods remain comparable. If data quality is compromised, we flag it in reporting instead of presenting misleading numbers.

Can you guarantee a specific amount of traffic or sales?

No one can honestly guarantee exact traffic or sales figures, because search engines, competitors and market demand are outside anyone's control. What we can commit to is clear KPI definitions, a transparent process, agreed deliverables and honest, verifiable reporting.

Are branded searches included in the results?

We report branded and non-branded performance separately. Non-branded growth is the clearest indicator of SEO impact, while branded traffic is mainly influenced by your overall brand awareness and marketing activities.
