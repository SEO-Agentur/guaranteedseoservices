Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you guarantee a #1 position in Google Maps?

No. Nobody controls Google's rankings, and anyone promising a #1 position should be treated with caution. Guaranteed local SEO means the scope, deliverables, timelines and KPIs are agreed in writing before work starts – and we report transparently against them.

How long does it take to see local SEO results?

Profile improvements often show first changes in Google Business Profile activity within 4–8 weeks. Stronger map pack positions in competitive areas usually take 3–6 months, depending on competition, profile history and reviews.

Do I need a physical address for local SEO?

Not necessarily. Service-area businesses can hide their address and define the areas they serve, as long as they meet customers in person. Virtual offices and mailboxes are not eligible for a Google Business Profile.

What if I have multiple locations?

Each location needs its own verified Google Business Profile and its own location page with unique content, consistent NAP details and location-specific reporting.

Which metrics should I focus on?

Focus on map pack visibility, profile interactions (website clicks, calls and direction requests), enquiries or bookings, organic traffic to location pages and the number and quality of your reviews.
