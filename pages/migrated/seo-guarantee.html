FAQ

Understand the terms before you sign.

Clear answers to common questions about our SEO guarantee framework.

More questions? Visit our FAQ →

Is this page the binding guarantee?

No. This page explains the framework. Binding commitments, exclusions and any remedies are defined in your signed agreement.

How are KPIs and baselines agreed?

Before work starts, we agree which KPIs matter for your goals, record the current baseline from an agreed data source (for example Search Console or Analytics) and set the review dates.

What exclusions may apply?

Typical exclusions cover factors outside our direct control, such as search engine algorithm changes, competitor activity, third-party platform decisions and delays in implementation on your side. Your agreement lists the exclusions that apply.

What happens if a commitment is missed?

We review the evidence together against the agreed definition. Any remedy — for example additional work or a refund — applies as set out in writing in your agreement.

How will progress be measured?

Against the agreed baseline, using the agreed data sources and reporting window. You receive regular progress reports at the cadence defined in your agreement.
